Katherine Johnson
SCIENCE
1918–2020 · NASA mathematician and aerospace technologist
ACHIEVEMENT
Katherine Johnson's precise orbital mechanics calculations were crucial to NASA's early space missions. John Glenn refused to fly unless Johnson personally verified the computer's calculations before his 1962 orbital flight.
💡
She calculated trajectories by hand so accurately that her work remained indispensable even after NASA introduced electronic computers.
📚TODAY'S LESSON
HISTORY
♡
The Underground Railroad: Freedom's Secret Network
The Underground Railroad was not a literal railroad but a vast, clandestine network of safe houses, secret routes, and brave conductors that helped enslaved African Americans escape to freedom between the 1780s and 1860s. Harriet Tubman made 13 dangerous missions back into the South, guiding approximately 70 people to freedom and never losing a single passenger.
WHY IT MATTERS
It stands as one of history's greatest acts of collective resistance — proof that ordinary people, acting with extraordinary courage, can dismantle systems of oppression.
